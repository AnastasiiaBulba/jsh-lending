Our Programs

Super Kickers (Ages 5-7)

A playful yet structured introduction to martial arts, focusing on coordination, balance, and basic self-defense techniques. Kids learn through interactive games, simple drills, and positive reinforcement.

Warrior Juniors (Ages 8-12)

A more structured program that builds fundamental martial arts techniques, discipline, and self-confidence. Students start practicing kata, controlled sparring, and flexibility training

Teen Fighters (Ages 13-17)

A dynamic program designed to refine techniques, enhance strength, and develop tactical combat skills. This program incorporates advanced self-defense strategies, endurance training, and mental exercises.
